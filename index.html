From our briefs

Bridging gaps in care for aging women

Health Policy team, Spring 2024

Women over the age of 65 represent one of the fastest-growing segments of the U.S. population, yet federal healthcare policy has historically treated aging and gender as separate analytic categories. This brief identifies structural gaps in Medicare coverage, community-based care availability, and preventive service delivery that disproportionately affect aging women — particularly those in rural areas and those from racial and ethnic minority communities.

The team recommends

  • Expand Medicare Preventive Service Coverage
  • Strengthen the Long-Term Care Workforce
  • Scale Community Health Navigator Programs
Read the brief

Building electrification in New England

Technology Policy team, Fall 2023

Buildings account for approximately 27% of greenhouse gas emissions in New England — among the highest regional shares in the United States — driven largely by natural gas heating in aging residential stock.

How a brief is made

Each team owns its research from the first question to publication. Agencies and experts working on the same issues sometimes share data and perspective along the way. The conclusions are the students' own.
