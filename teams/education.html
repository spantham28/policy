The Education Policy team investigated the structural forces shaping learning outcomes, from school climate and disciplinary disparities to pandemic recovery and global access.

This team is not running in Fall 2026. Its briefs remain available.

Focus areas

  • School climate and student well-being interventions
  • School-to-prison pipeline and disciplinary reform
  • Pandemic learning loss and equity-centered recovery
  • Global education access and international frameworks
  • School resource officer policy and safety
  • Teacher pipeline and workforce development
  • Higher education affordability and access

Research briefs

  • Education Policy teamFall 2023Landing page forthcoming

    Evaluating the Domestic Impact of Returned Peace Corps Volunteers: A Massachusetts Case Study

    Studies the civic and educational impact of returned Peace Corps volunteers as community educators, proposing federal incentives to direct this workforce toward under-resourced school districts.

  • Education Policy teamSpring 2023Landing page forthcoming

    School Resource Officers and the School-to-Prison Pipeline

    Analyzes the evidence on SRO programs and their disproportionate impact on students of color, proposing alternative school safety models grounded in restorative practice.

  • Education Policy teamFall 2022Open access

    Challenges and Opportunities in the COVID-19 Global Education Response

    Assesses how school systems worldwide responded to pandemic disruptions and identifies durable policy reforms for an equitable recovery in global education.

Who we have worked with

Our education research involved collaboration with Congressional education committees, state departments of education, urban school districts, and international organizations including UNICEF and the United Nations. It has informed curriculum policy, school safety legislation, and post-pandemic recovery planning.

Policy Program
