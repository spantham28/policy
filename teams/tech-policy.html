About the team

The Technology Policy team studies how significant technologies, particularly frontier AI models deployed in healthcare settings, intersect with regulatory frameworks for privacy. We work to ensure that legislation keeps pace with innovation while protecting the public interest.

Past focus areas

  • AI governance and algorithmic accountability
  • Genomic data privacy
  • Energy transitions
  • Regulatory frameworks for autonomous systems
  • Post-hoc de-identification of data
  • Digital equity

Who we have worked with

Our technology research has involved collaboration with Congressional offices on the Senate Commerce Committee, state energy offices, environmental nonprofits, and technology companies seeking regulatory clarity. Our work spans both domestic legislation and international technology governance.
