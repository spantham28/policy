About the team

The Health Policy team investigates systemic gaps in healthcare access, delivery, and funding across the United States, translating clinical and epidemiological evidence into concrete policy recommendations.

Past focus areas

  • Aging women's healthcare and Medicare reform
  • Mental health screenings and youth behavioral health
  • Fentanyl epidemic and substance use policy
  • Breast cancer prevention and early detection access
  • Community health infrastructure in rural areas
  • Maternal mortality and reproductive health equity
  • Public health emergency preparedness

Research briefs

  • Health Policy teamSpring 2024Open access

    Bridging Gaps in Care: Strategic Policy Initiatives for Enhancing Health Care Services for Aging Women

    Evaluates federal and state-level gaps in healthcare delivery for women over 65, with recommendations for Medicare reform, community-based care, and preventive service coverage expansion.

  • Health Policy teamFall 2023Landing page forthcoming

    Mental Health Screenings and Youth: A Policy Framework for School-Based Intervention

    Analyzes universal mental health screening programs in K–12 schools and proposes a tiered federal framework for school-district implementation.

  • Health Policy teamSpring 2023Landing page forthcoming

    Addressing the Fentanyl Epidemic: Harm Reduction Policy at the State Level

    Examines harm reduction strategies — naloxone access, supervised consumption sites, drug checking services — and evaluates legislative pathways across high-burden states.

Who we have worked with

Our health research has involved collaboration with U.S. Congressional offices, state health departments, community health organizations, and international agencies including the World Health Organization.
