Good collaborations

The best collaborations start from an issue an office is genuinely working on. If you are unsure whether yours fits, send it anyway.

  • A topic, not an assignmentTell us about an issue your office is working on. Our teams decide what to study and how.
  • A scope that fits one semesterSpecific enough that a team of undergraduates can research it well in about three months.
  • People who can share what they knowStaff who can talk with students, point them to data or make introductions.
  • Openness to independent findingsOur briefs are the students' own analysis, published openly under the program's name. We are glad to share them with you.
